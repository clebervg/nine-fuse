This policy explains what data the NineFuse app (Android and iOS) uses, why, and who it is shared with. The responsible party is [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] (“we”), contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

1. Summary

  • NineFuse has no sign-up, no login and no server of our own. We do not ask for your name, email, phone, location or contacts.
  • Your game progress stays only on your device.
  • The game shows Google AdMob ads, which may collect device identifiers (see section 3).

2. Data stored on your device

To make the game work we store locally: current level, stars, high scores, coins, item counts (hammer, bomb, brush), chests already opened and the time of the last daily spin. This data is not sent to us or to third parties and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads (videos you choose to watch in exchange for coins, items or extra moves) through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use, under the Google Privacy Policy:

  • the device advertising identifier (Advertising ID);
  • IP address and general device and system information (model, version, language);
  • ad interaction and app performance data.

For this activity Google acts as an independent controller. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Brazil), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset or disable the advertising identifier and personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy › Apple Advertising and Tracking).

4. Notifications

The app may send local notifications (daily spin and come-back reminders). They are scheduled on the device itself, with no data sent to servers. You can turn them off at any time in system settings.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. The only sharing is the one described in section 3, with Google AdMob, to show ads.

6. Children

NineFuse is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child provided data, contact us.

7. Your rights

Since we do not keep personal data about you, there is no data of ours to access or delete; game data is removed by uninstalling the app. For data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights (access, correction, deletion, objection, portability, under GDPR and LGPD) through Google’s tools at myaccount.google.com. For any question, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

We may update this policy (for example when adding new features). The current version is always on this page, with the date at the top.
